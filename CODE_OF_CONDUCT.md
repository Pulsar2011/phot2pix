# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, religion, sexual orientation, or any other personal characteristic.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate toward other contributors and users.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask for clarification before assuming bad intent.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people whose first language differs from yours.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or derogatory comments.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Threats or intimidation, in any form.
- Publishing others' private information (address, email, etc.) without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, comments, and any other channel used for the project. It also applies when an individual represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor whose behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

## Reporting

To report a violation, contact the maintainers at **<contact-email>**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and treated confidentially. Maintainers are expected to respect the privacy and safety of the person reporting an incident.
